Our Mission

Understanding. Empathy. Action.

Why we exist, what we're working towards, and the three steps we believe lead to lasting change.

The Problem

In the world, thousands of people face discrimination and hate just because of something they can't control and something that should be celebrated—their culture. Stereotypes are created and imposed on people based on a lack of understanding, which not only creates stereotypes, but also creates hostile environments and distance between certain groups.

The Goal

Heritage in Harmony hopes to bridge the gap between different communities and act as a connecting piece, allowing people to recognize their differences and, rather than letting them set them apart, having them bring them together. Our main goal is to answer this question:

“How will having the proper understanding of experiences different from others allow us to create a more integrated community where people are less likely to discriminate against others?”

Three Pillars

The steps we believe lead to lasting change.

01

Understanding

The first essential step towards creating this change involves recognizing and becoming aware of the differences that exist in our community—specifically, in different cultures. For example, the food, music, or language that is part of a culture. Understanding this is necessary for creating a safe and welcoming environment where everyone feels free to speak their mind.

02

Empathy

The next step towards our goal includes feeling connected to and identifying with these different cultures. Instead of trying to relate to the differences and find similarities between yourself and other communities, it is important to acknowledge the differences and realize the struggles people might face. Empathizing with the people around us is a key step towards the final goal, as empathy makes this environment possible.

03

Action

The last and final step in this process is action. Rather than just thinking about these things and reflecting upon them, we want to turn awareness into manageable steps, focusing on creating more inclusive communities. Action ties everything together and drives actual change.
