Get Involved

How to get involved

There are many ways to support Heritage in Harmony—pick the one that fits you.

1

Attend an event

Join us in person to connect with others and learn more about different cultures and experiences.

2

Share your story

Anonymously submit a personal experience or piece of your culture for others to learn from.

Share your story →
3

Educate yourself by reading an article

Take time to learn about the issues different communities face and how discrimination impacts them.

4

Volunteer

Lend your time and skills to help plan events, collect stories, or spread the word.

5

Collaborate with H in H

Partner with us as an organization, club, or individual working towards the same goals.
