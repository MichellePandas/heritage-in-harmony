Heritage in Harmony

Different backgrounds. Different experiences. One community.

Heritage in Harmony is a student-led initiative dedicated to raising awareness about discrimination against cultures, amplifying perspectives that often go unheard, and creating a greater understanding of the people around us who differ in experiences and cultural values.
