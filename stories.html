Perspectives & Stories

Every culture has a story worth passing on.

A place to read, reflect, and share—without judgement.

Various cultures exist, each with their own values and narratives they hope to pass on to others. There are hundreds of stories and perspectives that deserve to be heard, and in order to fulfill this need, we ask everyone to think about their own stories and ask themselves: what stories do I have, and how can I pass that on to the next person?

We want students to have a place where they are able to share these experiences without judgement. Here, people can anonymously submit a specific story or experience they have—a story that has been part of their culture that they're hoping to pass on to other people, or an experience that occurred that they want other people to hear, so they have someone to relate to.

Ready to share yours?

Your submission is anonymous, and can be as short or as long as you'd like.
