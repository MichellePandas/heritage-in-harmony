About Us

Welcome to Heritage in Harmony

An initiative working on preventing hate and discrimination against targeted communities.

Welcome to Heritage in Harmony, an initiative working on preventing hate and discrimination against targeted communities. We believe that understanding is a key step towards creating a safe space in communities, challenging stereotypes, and turning awareness into action.

Founder

Sophie Wang is a 10th-grade student who looks for injustice in her community, hoping to correct it. She founded Heritage in Harmony as a way to raise awareness and educate people on an issue that exists in many ways across many communities.
